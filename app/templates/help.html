Using the Lab Map

Your lab's floor plan, with its fridges, freezers, cabinets and equipment marked. Click a freezer to see the stocks kept in it; click a machine to see today's bookings and book it.

Finding something

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move about; ⤢ shows the whole floor again.
  • Type in Find: places whose name, room or kind match light up, the rest fade. Enter goes to the first.
  • The coloured chips are the kinds of place on the map: click one to hide or show them all.
  • The list beside the map is by room. Click a place there, or on the map, to open it.
  • 🔗 Link copies an address that opens the map on that place, to put in a protocol or the notebook.

What a place shows

  • Fridges, freezers, cabinets: the stock records (glycerol stocks, primers, chemicals, antibodies, plant material) whose place is this one, from Lab Stocks. You see what Lab Stocks lets you see.
  • Equipment linked to Booking: whether it is free now, today's bookings, and Book it, which opens its calendar.

Making the map (lab admins)

  1. Upload a floor plan (＋ beside the map's name). A PDF from the architect or facilities is best: the fridges, freezers, cabinets and equipment its labels name are offered as suggestions. A picture works too; you then draw every place yourself.
  2. Tick the suggestions that are your lab's and add them. They come with a name and the nearest room; check both.
  3. Draw a box or Draw an outline for anything else. Choose a place and drag it, or its corners, to put it right.
  4. For a machine, choose what it is Booked in Booking as. For storage, older stock records that say where they are in words can be found by those words (Older stock records call it).

A new version of the drawing (Map settings) keeps the places where they were on the page, so a revised plan of the same floor needs no re-marking.
