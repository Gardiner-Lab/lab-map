Each lab's floor plans, with the fridges, freezers, cabinets and equipment on them: what is kept where, from Lab Stocks, and what is booked when, from Booking. How to use it →

Who sees what

A map is its lab's: the lab's members see it, its lab admins upload and mark it, and the suite's administrators can do both for any lab. The stocks and bookings a place shows come from Lab Stocks and Booking as they would for you there, so a map never shows more than those tools do.

Where the data is kept

In the suite's map-data volume, in one database, map.db: the maps, the places on them, and each drawing. It is in the suite's nightly backups with the other tools' databases.
